# OpenVR loaders

The existing loaders are unmodified Valve OpenVR v2.12.14 binaries.
Their SHA-256 hashes were compared with fresh downloads from the official tag
on 2026-10-07 and match byte-for-byte. No local native rebuild was performed.

- Windows x64: https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr/blob/v2.12.14/bin/win64/openvr_api.dll
- Linux x64: https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr/blob/v2.12.14/bin/linux64/libopenvr_api.so
